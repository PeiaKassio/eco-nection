Representation in numbers

Every panel follows the filters on the atlas. Art and science collections differ in size, so comparisons default to shares within each dataset.

Time

Attention over time, with key events

Lines: yearly share of records per cluster, averaged over 3 years (years with fewer than 5 records are left out). Grey bars: records per year, which shows how unevenly the collection grows. Triangles: key events.

Per capita

Artworks per 1M inhabitants

Countries with at least 1M inhabitants and at least 2 artworks. Small countries with an active scene rise to the top.

MAP · where

Mapbox globe or flat map. Artworks sit at their recorded location, publications at the centre of their study area; points at the same spot are fanned out slightly. Studies without a mappable area (global or multi-country) cannot be shown on the map. Country colour compares a country's share of all artworks with its share of placed publications.

TIME · when

X is the year. Artworks sit in the upper band, publications in the lower band. In the middle, each cluster is drawn twice: a filled dot at the median year of its artworks and a ring at the median year of its publications (at least 3 records each). The line between them is the time gap. Vertical lines are key events.

LINKS · art ↔ science

A resonance links an artwork and a publication that share a topic cluster, the same country or continent, and are at most 5 years apart. Each artwork keeps its 3 strongest links. Shared topics and the same country make a link stronger.

TOPICS · what

The only force layout left: topics that often appear on the same record pull together. Distance carries meaning only as "used together", the axes carry none.

Events

A curated list of 38 political, scientific, cultural and disaster events. In this mockup dates and links come from general knowledge and still need checking against sources.

About this mockup

Built from a snapshot of artwork-data.json, science-map.json, countryPopulation.json and Natural Earth country borders (world-atlas 110m) for the country colours. Darker cluster colours were brightened for the dark background.
