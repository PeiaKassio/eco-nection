Analysis / Index

What the network shows

Every panel follows the filters on the map. Comparisons use shares within each dataset, because the art and science collections differ in size.

Findings

Patterns to look at more closely

Generated by rules from the current selection. Click one to filter the map.

Gap

Art vs. science attention

Share of artworks and of publications that touch each cluster. The label shows the gap in percentage points.

Time

Attention over time

Yearly share of records per cluster, averaged over 3 years. Years with fewer than 5 records are left out.

Clustering

Detected communities vs. curated clusters

The Louvain algorithm groups topics by how often they appear together on the same record, without knowing the curated clusters. Each card shows one detected community and which curated clusters its topics come from.

Co-occurrence

Which clusters appear together

Colour is the Jaccard overlap (shared records ÷ records with either cluster). The number is the count of shared records.

Bridges

Topics that connect clusters

Betweenness centrality in the topic network: how often a topic lies on the shortest path between two other topics.

Places

Top countries by main cluster

Each record counts once, under the cluster of its first topic. For science, the place is the study area.

Network

Each artwork and publication links to its topics, its country and (for art) its art forms. Topics link to their curated cluster. Artists are hidden by default because most have a single work.

Clusters & communities

Curated clusters come from topicClusters.json. Communities are found with the Louvain method, which maximises modularity: more links inside groups than chance would predict.

Shares

A share is the number of records with a cluster divided by all records of that dataset in the selection. A record can carry several clusters, so shares can add up to more than 100%.

About this mockup

Built from a snapshot of artwork-data.json and science-map.json. The darkest cluster colours were brightened so they stay visible on the dark background.
