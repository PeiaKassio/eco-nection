How the comparison is calculated

Artworks and scientific publications come from different corpora, so Compare mode can use each dataset's internal share rather than raw counts. Single-dataset views use absolute counts by default.

Topic share: available in Compare mode. It divides unique matching records for a topic cluster by all unique records in the current geography and time base. The selected topic changes the numerator, not the denominator.

Yearly share: unique topic records in a year divided by all unique records in that same year, geography, and dataset.

Topic difference: Art share minus Science share, shown in percentage points.

Attention center: the weighted median year of the yearly share series. Temporal shift is Art attention center minus Science attention center.

Records may belong to several topic clusters. They count once per cluster and once in the denominator, so cluster shares can sum above 100%.

Science geography means study area, not author affiliation. Sparse data can prevent reliable temporal-shift estimates.

Per population: matching records divided by the combined population of the selected countries, multiplied by 1 million. With All countries, the denominator is the population sum for all countries in the active continent filter, or all known countries when no continent is selected. Missing population is shown as unavailable.
