How the comparison is calculated

Artworks and scientific publications come from different corpora, so the default comparison uses each dataset's internal share rather than raw counts.

Topic share: unique matching records for a topic cluster divided by all unique records in the current geography and time base. The selected topic changes the numerator, not the denominator.

Yearly share: unique topic records in a year divided by all unique records in that same year, geography, and dataset.

Topic difference: Art share minus Science share, shown in percentage points.

Attention center: the weighted median year of the yearly share series. Temporal shift is Art attention center minus Science attention center.

Records may belong to several topic clusters. They count once per cluster and once in the denominator, so cluster shares can sum above 100%.

Science geography means study area, not author affiliation. Sparse data can prevent reliable temporal-shift estimates.

Per-population mode uses country population values where available and reports records per 1 million inhabitants. Missing population is shown as unavailable.
